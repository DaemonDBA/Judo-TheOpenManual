# Yoko Otoshi (Side Drop)

> **Source:** Technique Description and Breakdown sourced from *Kodokan Judo*, pg. 78

---

## Technique Description
> "Breaking your opponent's balance to his right side, you place your left foot outside his right foot and throw him to your left by dropping to your left side."

---

## Technique Breakdown
1. Begin in the right natural posture.
2. Step to your right and try to break your opponent's balance to his left.
3. He resists by shifting his weight to his right.
4. Place your left foot at the outside of his right ankle and drop to your left.
5. As you fall, pull to your left with both hands.
6. Your opponent will land almost parallel to you.

---

## Technique Overview

| Category | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Hands** | Standard lapel and sleeve grip. When falling, pull with both hands upwards and towards the throw. |
| **Feet** | Your left foot with go outside of his right. Your right foot will fall naturally between their right and left legs. |
| **Timing** | Opponent must be shifting their weight forward for full effect. |
| **Variations** | There are entry/footwork differences for various angles, but the throw is the same. |

### Combinations
* **As a starting technique:**
  * Yoko Otoshi to Ne Waza
* **As a finishing technique:**
  * O Uchi Gari to Yoko Otoshi

### Counters
* **If your opponent senses that this technique is coming and drops their weight lower than yours, it will be difficult or impossible to successfully land this technique. Their position would then allow them to enter into Ne Waza.

## Tactical / Tournament Usage
The trigger for this technique is to get your opponent's weight forward and on one side. Set up the weight balance and position, then disappear beneath them with the drop.

### Body Type Differences
* **Height:** If your opponent is much taller than you, pay extra attention to the pull when falling. Otherwise, you will lose your grip and the throw will fail.
* **Weight:** If your opponent is heavier and tends to drop their weight lower in defense, you will either need to catch them through setups or consider a different technique.

### Safety
* Once the throw is executed, release your lapel grip, so that the opponent can slap the mat.
* Supporting your opponent’s weight through the fall by pulling the sleeve grip up slightly at the end will lessen the impact.
